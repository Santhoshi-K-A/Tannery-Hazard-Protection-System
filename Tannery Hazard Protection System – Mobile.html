Interactive model equations

dC/dt = S(t) − (k + u·k_v)·C
dy/dt = (C − y)/τ + σ·ξ(t)
u = 1 when fan on and not failed

Ventilation is modelled only as extra dilution/extraction; it does not destroy or neutralise gas. Single well-mixed zone per gas; no spatial transport.

Limitations

Computational representation of a proposed invention — not a certified gas-monitoring or emergency device. Simulated fault responses do not prove real-world reliability. Emergency messages direct people to site procedures and qualified personnel. The MQ-4 is methane-oriented and is not used for NH₃/H₂S here; gas-specific sensors are assumed.
